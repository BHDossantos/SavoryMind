# PROJECT — CasaFlow (AvailableNow Home)

## One-line pitch
Trusted help at home, available now — a real-time home & local services
marketplace where customers find, book, and pay verified providers for
cleaning, plumbing, handyman, moving, and repair work.

## Vision
Home services today are run on WhatsApp, phone calls, and informal
referrals. Customers don't know who to trust, can't tell what a fair
price looks like, and waste hours coordinating. Providers chase bad
leads and lack basic digital tools.

CasaFlow makes home services as bookable as a restaurant table:
search → see verified pros with prices and availability → request
quote or book instantly → track status → pay → review.

## Why now
- Post-pandemic shift to flexible, on-demand local services.
- Airbnb / short-term rental hosts need recurring cleaning and
  emergency repairs and will pay for speed.
- Independent providers (cleaners, plumbers, handymen) still operate
  off paper and SMS — a clear gap to digitize.
- Cross-border city populations (expats, remote workers, students)
  need a multilingual flow that local-language platforms miss.

## Core promise
> Need help at home? Find trusted local pros available today.

## Top problems we solve
1. Trust — verified providers, identity checks, real reviews.
2. Availability — real-time job feed, today/urgent/emergency tiers.
3. Pricing transparency — quote ranges, fixed-price packages, fee
   breakdown before booking.
4. Scattered coordination — structured job requests, photos, in-app
   chat replace WhatsApp threads.
5. Bad leads — qualified requests, customer reliability scores,
   deposits for urgent jobs.
6. No-shows — cancellation policies, ratings, two-sided reliability.
7. Hard-to-describe jobs — guided request forms with photos and
   property context.
8. No digital tooling for small providers — calendar, quotes,
   invoices, payments in one app.
9. Useless comparisons — compare by availability, distance, price,
   verified skill, language, completion history.
10. Emergencies — dedicated urgent dispatch flow.
11. Repeat service for hosts/landlords — recurring bookings and
    property profiles.
12. Language barriers — multilingual flow and translated messages.
13. Inconsistent payment — in-app deposits/payments, invoices,
    platform-fee tracking.
14. Weak quality control — platform quality score, dispute process,
    suspension levers.

## Strongest wedge
Airbnb and short-term rental hosts. They need repeat cleaning, urgent
repairs, and reliable providers; they're willing to pay for speed.

## Launch market & categories
- **City:** Rome.
- **MVP categories:** Cleaning, Handyman, Plumbing, Furniture assembly.
- **Initial supply target:**
  - 20 cleaners
  - 20 handymen
  - 10 plumbers
  - 10 furniture-assembly providers

## Go-to-market
- For customers: "Trusted home help, booked without calling."
- For providers: "Get paid jobs without chasing WhatsApp leads."
- For Airbnb hosts: "Turnover cleaning and maintenance handled in
  one place."

## Slogan
**Trusted help at home, available now.**

## Non-goals (MVP)
- No full electrical/gas/HVAC work until license verification is
  hardened.
- No native mobile apps yet — mobile-first PWA only.
- No multi-city expansion until Rome reaches healthy liquidity.
- No background-check integrations beyond document upload + admin
  review.

## Success criteria
- 60 active providers in Rome within 90 days of launch.
- ≥ 70% of submitted jobs receive at least one quote within 4 hours.
- ≥ 40% of jobs convert from request → booking.
- ≥ 4.5 average rating across completed bookings.
- ≥ 30% of bookings are repeat customers within 60 days (driven by
  cleaning + Airbnb hosts).
